# Short #4 — Stalin's blank logbook (supports the long video "Stalin's Logbook Recorded Every Minute. Two Days Are Blank.")

**Myth:** Stalin froze/collapsed the moment Hitler invaded.
**Truth (per the Kremlin office visitor log):** he received visitors every day from 22 June; the log is blank on 29–30 June, after Minsk fell (28 June). On 30 June his inner circle came to his dacha; Mikoyan believed Stalin thought they had come to arrest him. That day the State Defense Committee (GKO) was created with Stalin as chairman.

## Script (to record, ~38s)
```
Everyone says Stalin froze when Hitler invaded. His own logbook says he didn't.

June 22nd, 1941. From the first morning of the invasion, Stalin's Kremlin office never empties. Generals, ministers, name after name.

Then, on June 28th, Minsk falls.

June 29th. June 30th. The logbook is blank. Two days. Not a single name.

Stalin has gone to his dacha outside Moscow.

On the second day, his inner circle drives out to see him. Mikoyan later wrote that Stalin seemed to think they'd come to arrest him.

They hadn't. They came to put him in charge of the entire war.

What happened in those two missing days is the real story.
```

## Fact notes
- Kremlin office visitor log (published in *Istoricheskii Arkhiv*, "Na prieme u Stalina"): entries 22–28 June 1941, none on 29–30 June.
- Minsk entered by German forces 28 June 1941.
- Dacha visit and Mikoyan's impression of arrest fear: Mikoyan's memoirs (via Spartacus Educational).
- GKO founded 30 June 1941, Stalin chairman, Molotov deputy (Wikipedia: State Defense Committee).
- Not used: the often-quoted "5:45 a.m." first entry on 22 June — not verified in this research pass.
